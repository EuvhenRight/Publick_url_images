# Medical AI output review checklist

Use this checklist when reviewing a medical AI draft, summary, or follow-up note.

## Before reading
- [ ] I know the source material the AI used.
- [ ] I know whether this is a draft, summary, or recommendation.
- [ ] I am not treating polished wording as proof of correctness.

## Content check
- [ ] The note matches the patient context.
- [ ] No key history, medication, lab, or timeline detail is missing.
- [ ] There are no contradictions with the chart.
- [ ] Uncertainty is stated clearly, not hidden.
- [ ] The model did not overstate confidence.

## Clinical safety check
- [ ] I can identify what still needs human verification.
- [ ] Any recommendation can be traced back to the chart or evidence provided.
- [ ] I would not make a final decision based on this text alone.
- [ ] If the output were wrong, I would be able to explain why.

## Questions to ask the model
If the AI output feels too complete, ask it:

- What assumptions are you making?
- What information is missing from the source material?
- Which part of this summary is least certain?
- What could a clinician disagree with here?
- What should be checked in the chart before using this?

## Good rule of thumb
If a junior clinician would likely accept the output at face value, it needs stronger safeguards.
If a senior clinician would immediately treat it as a draft, the workflow is probably healthier.

## Closing reminder
AI can save time, but in medicine the real value is not speed alone.
It is speed plus a reliable human review step.

# Prompts: Reasoning About AI Wearable Recording

Reusable prompts for exploring the privacy/consent issues raised in the post. Paste into any capable LLM.

## 1. Balanced debate generator

```
You are a neutral moderator. Debate the question:
"Should always-on recording smart glasses be legal to wear in public spaces?"

Produce:
1. Three strongest arguments FOR, each with a concrete example.
2. Three strongest arguments AGAINST, each with a concrete example.
3. Key legal concepts at stake (consent regimes, expectation of privacy, audio vs video).
4. A one-paragraph synthesis identifying where reasonable policy might land.

Do not take a side. Flag any claim that would need jurisdiction-specific legal verification.
```

## 2. Personal risk assessment (wearer)

```
I am considering wearing AI glasses that can record audio and video and transcribe conversations.
My primary state/country is: [FILL IN].
I travel frequently to: [FILL IN].

Walk me through:
- Whether my location(s) use one-party or all-party consent for private conversations.
- Situations where I would most likely violate a recording/eavesdropping law.
- Practical habits that keep me compliant.
End with: "This is not legal advice; verify current statutes with a licensed attorney."
```

## 3. Bystander script builder

```
Help me politely but firmly ask someone to stop recording with their smart glasses.
Context: [casual social / professional meeting / private home].
Give me:
- A one-line polite version.
- A firmer version if they refuse.
- A version that references that we're in an all-party-consent jurisdiction.
Keep each under 30 words.
```

## 4. Fact-check my post claims

```
Review these claims from a social media post about AI wearables and flag which are
(a) accurate, (b) oversimplified, or (c) needing a date/jurisdiction caveat:

1. "Twelve US states require everyone in a private conversation to agree before recording."
2. "Smart glasses that transcribe nonstop can break decades-old wiretapping laws just by being switched on near you."
3. "California only introduced its first bill aimed at wearable recording in early 2026, and it still isn't law."

For each, explain the nuance a careful reader should know.
```

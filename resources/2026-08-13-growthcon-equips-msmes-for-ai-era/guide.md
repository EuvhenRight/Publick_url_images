# GrowthCon MSME AI Starter Guide

This guide turns the post’s idea into a simple first step: pick one repetitive workflow, add AI to the first draft, and keep a human review before sending anything out.

## Best fit
Use this approach when a task:
- Happens often
- Follows clear rules
- Uses existing information
- Can be checked quickly by a person
- Does not require high-stakes judgment on every step

## Good starter workflows
### 1) Customer replies
Examples:
- Pricing questions
- Order status updates
- Meeting confirmations
- Frequently asked questions

### 2) Invoice follow-up
Examples:
- Overdue payment reminders
- Polite nudges after a due date
- Payment link follow-ups
- Second or third reminder drafts

## A simple 4-step rollout
### Step 1: Choose one task
Pick the task that is:
- Repetitive
- Easy to review
- Time-consuming enough to matter

A good test: can a team member check the AI draft in under 2 minutes?

### Step 2: Define the rules
Write down:
- What the message must include
- What it must not say
- Tone of voice
- Approval owner
- When it should be escalated to a person

### Step 3: Use AI for the first draft only
Do not ask AI to send messages directly at first. Instead:
- Feed it the context
- Ask for a draft
- Review and edit
- Send manually

### Step 4: Measure the time saved
Track:
- Number of items handled
- Time before AI
- Time after AI
- Error rate or edits needed

Even a small improvement matters. Saving 10 minutes on 15 items a day is 150 minutes, or 2.5 hours.

## What to keep human
Keep a person in the loop when:
- The customer is upset
- The invoice is disputed
- The situation is unusual
- The message could create a legal or financial risk
- The answer depends on policy exceptions

## Guardrails
- Never paste sensitive data unless your organization allows it
- Review every customer-facing draft before sending
- Use approved templates and business facts only
- Update prompts when policies change

## Success criteria for the first month
A good pilot should show:
- Faster drafting
- Fewer blank-page moments
- Consistent tone
- No increase in avoidable mistakes

## Recommended first owner
Assign one person who:
- Knows the workflow
- Can spot errors quickly
- Has authority to improve the template

## Example starting point
If your business wants a quick win, start with:
1. Customer replies if your team handles lots of repeated questions
2. Invoice follow-up if overdue reminders take too long

## Decision checklist
Choose the workflow that is:
- Repeated at least daily or weekly
- Easy to verify
- Based on standard language
- Worth 30+ minutes per week

If it fails those tests, pick a simpler task.

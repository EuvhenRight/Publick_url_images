# Prompt: Audit Hiring or Review Notes for Appearance Bias

Use this prompt with an AI assistant to review interview feedback, promotion notes, or performance review drafts for appearance-coded language.

Do not include confidential, personal, medical, legally sensitive, or identifying information unless your organization has approved that use. Replace names with labels such as Candidate A or Employee B.

## Prompt

You are helping audit workplace evaluation notes for possible appearance bias and vague subjective language.

Task:
1. Identify phrases that may reflect appearance bias, lookism, or subjective standards such as “polish,” “fit,” “image,” “professional look,” “executive presence,” or “client-ready.”
2. Explain why each phrase could be problematic.
3. Rewrite each phrase into job-related, behavior-based language.
4. Flag any comment that may overlap with protected characteristics such as race, sex, religion, disability, age, national origin, pregnancy, or gender identity/expression.
5. Do not decide whether discrimination occurred. Focus on improving clarity, fairness, and job relevance.
6. If there is not enough evidence to support a statement, say what evidence would be needed.

Evaluation criteria to use:
- Is the comment tied to a written job requirement?
- Is it based on observable behavior or measurable work output?
- Would the same standard be applied consistently to every candidate or employee?
- Could the comment penalize someone for body size, hairstyle, grooming norms, clothing, tattoos, piercings, skin, teeth, age appearance, disability, or gender expression?
- Can the feedback be rewritten without referring to appearance?

Return your answer in this format:

## Potentially biased or vague language

| Original phrase | Concern | Better job-related wording | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|

## Comments that should be reviewed by HR or legal counsel

List any comments that may involve protected characteristics or legal risk. Do not provide legal conclusions.

## Revised evaluation notes

Provide a cleaned-up version that uses job-related evidence only.

Text to audit:

[PASTE NOTES HERE]

## Example

Input:

Candidate A was smart but did not seem polished enough for clients. Their style was not really aligned with our brand, and I am not sure they have executive presence.

Possible output:

| Original phrase | Concern | Better job-related wording | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| “not polished enough for clients” | Vague and may reflect appearance, class, accent, or style preferences rather than job ability. | “The candidate did not provide a clear example of managing a difficult client conversation.” | Notes from the answer, client-facing requirements, and scoring rubric. |
| “style was not aligned with our brand” | Could be an appearance-based judgment unless tied to a written, consistently applied standard. | “No job-related concern documented.” | Written dress, uniform, or presentation requirement, if applicable. |
| “executive presence” | Vague and often inconsistently applied. | “The candidate’s answer did not show experience influencing senior stakeholders.” | Specific examples from the interview response. |

# The Judgment Loop: A Practical Guide to Deciding What's Worth Building

The LinkedIn post argues that AI has made every step of the build loop nearly free — except one: deciding *what* deserves your time. This guide turns that idea into a repeatable process, so you can build the judgment the old apprenticeship path used to hand you for free.

## The Loop

```
Learn → Build → Launch → Measure → Kill or Double Down → Repeat
```

AI collapses the cost of Learn, Build, and Launch. Your edge is now concentrated in **choosing the input** and **reading the output honestly**.

## Part 1: Deciding What's Worth Building

Before you spend a Saturday, run the idea through these five filters. Write one honest sentence for each.

1. **Pain** — Whose problem is this, and how do you know it hurts? (Name a real person, not "people.")
2. **Frequency** — How often does the pain occur? Daily beats yearly.
3. **Existing behavior** — What are they doing today to cope? If the answer is "nothing," the pain may not be real.
4. **Your unfair advantage** — Why you? Access, skill, insight, or distribution.
5. **Cheapest test** — What's the smallest thing that proves or kills the idea in under a day?

If you can't fill in #1 and #5 with specifics, don't build yet.

## Part 2: Building Judgment Deliberately

Since AI eats the junior rungs, you have to manufacture the reps you used to get from a first job. Three habits:

- **Predict before you ship.** Write down what you expect to happen (users, feedback, metrics). Compare to reality. The gap is your judgment lesson.
- **Keep a decision log.** Every time you choose to build/kill something, record why. Review monthly. Patterns emerge.
- **Ship in public and take the friction.** Feedback from strangers is the modern apprenticeship. Don't skip it.

## Part 3: A One-Weekend Cadence

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| Sat AM | Pick idea, run the 5 filters, write decision-log entry |
| Sat PM | Build the cheapest testable version with AI assistance |
| Sun AM | Launch to 5–20 real potential users |
| Sun PM | Measure against your predictions, decide: kill or continue |

## The Honest Kill Criteria

Decide the kill line *before* launching, so emotion can't move the goalposts:

- No one you don't know engages within a week → kill.
- You can't articulate the pain to a stranger in one sentence → pause and refine.
- You dread working on it and there's no external pull → kill.

## The Point

Speed is now a commodity. Judgment — knowing what to start — is the scarce asset. This loop is how you build that judgment on purpose instead of waiting for a job to give it to you.
